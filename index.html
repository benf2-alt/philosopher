About Hypatia

Hypatia of Alexandria was a Greek mathematician, astronomer, and philosopher who lived in Alexandria, Egypt, around 350–415 CE. She became one of the most famous scholars of the ancient world.

Her Life

Hypatia taught mathematics and philosophy at the famous city of Alexandria. She was respected for her intelligence and attracted students from across the Roman Empire.

She taught her students to think logically, question assumptions, search for evidence, and study mathematics in order to understand the universe.

Legacy

Today, Hypatia is remembered as a symbol of learning, scientific inquiry, and the pursuit of knowledge. Her life has inspired books, films, and historians around the world.
